## how these map to what ships

- the six-dice-plus-bay layout in these photos is the same arrangement as the `nocoin`
  variant. only the outside size changed, 66 x 88 -> 67 x 91, so those shots are
  representative.
- the coin layout in these photos is NOT the `coin` variant. the old one had a coin well,
  two counter wells and two dice. `coin` is seven dice plus the well, with no counter
  wells at all. a photo of the old one would show a layout nobody can download.

## lighting

shot under blue/purple rgb. the filament is blue but reads violet, and the white balance
is a long way off. worth reshooting under neutral light before these go on a listing.
